Where we work

Six doors, one practice.

You do not have to know which one is yours. Most people end up moving between two or three, sequenced to what their system can hold.

The wingIndividual Healing Nervous system healing, trauma, EMDR and somatic work, and neurofeedback support. Enter → The wingBetrayal Trauma Healing For partners. The panic, the intrusive thoughts, the hypervigilance. Trauma responses, not overreactions. Enter → The wingAddiction Recovery Porn and sex, love, relationship, and the behavioral addictions underneath. Coping that got stuck on repeat. Enter → The wingRelational Healing Couples work, formal disclosure, and repair, sequenced so it does not begin before both people are steady. Enter → The toolHypnotherapy A gentle pathway into the subconscious, to reach what the mind alone cannot. Enter → The toolBreathwork Somatic work that rewires the nervous system from the inside. Behind Thrive Beyond Trauma Counseling

Healing should feel human.

Founded by Akanksha Madan, LPC, CSAT, CMAT, CCPS, ERCEM, on one belief, and it is the standard every clinician here is held to.

“I help people come home to themselves, to laugh again, to belong again, and to know that healing doesn’t end at survival. It begins with thriving.”

What began as survival became a mission rooted in belonging and emotional reclamation. Here, we do not guide you toward becoming someone new. We guide you back to who you have always been.
